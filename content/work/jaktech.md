# Jaktech: A B2B product taken from 100 to 500 customers in a year

**Role:** Marketing Manager, promoted from Digital Marketer after 8 months
**Client:** Jaktech Engineering & Trading PLC. In-house SaaS and ERP products.
**Period:** September 2022 to June 2024
**Keywords woven:** startup-stage product, community, partnerships, budget discipline

## Challenge

Jaktech's flagship B2B product was stuck near 100 customers. Marketing budget was tight, the product competed against established players, and growth had to come from execution rather than spend.

## Result first, because it's the point

- The product grew from 100 to 500 customers within one year
- I contributed to roughly 60% of high-value client acquisition through marketing and direct sales
- Ran ads for the in-house products Medascore and Yeabrak and grew them to 70,000 followers across 4 platforms with minimal spend
- I was promoted to Marketing Manager after 8 months

## Approach

Three plays did the work.

**Direct acquisition.** I paired marketing with direct sales on the accounts that mattered, building proposals and campaigns around specific high-value prospects rather than broadcasting to everyone.

**Community on minimal spend.** I ran ads for Medascore and Yeabrak and grew them to 70,000 followers across 4 platforms through relentless content and audience building. That community became a standing distribution channel every later campaign could tap.

**Partnerships.** Jaktech's products touched development-sector work, so I managed campaign collaborations with stakeholders including GIZ, Sequa, and Menschen für Menschen. Those partnerships put the products in front of audiences no ad budget could reach.

*This is the engagement where I learned that distribution you own beats distribution you rent.*
