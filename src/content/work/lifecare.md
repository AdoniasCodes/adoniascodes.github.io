## Why this case study is different

Most portfolio case studies show you the trophy. This one shows you the work, one month in, with real numbers small enough to be believable. I publish it because how a marketer starts an engagement tells you more than a polished end result. The engagement also came the best way work comes: a referral, from a corporate website I had built for an international aerial-survey company.

## Challenge

Lifecare's website was completely offline. They had lost domain and hosting continuity, the old design was outdated, and there was no SEO strategy or internal technical expertise to lean on. In a market where hospitals, pharmacies, and procurement teams search before they buy, the company was invisible, and ranked for none of its 30 target keywords.

## Approach

First I rebuilt the site itself: a fresh, responsive WordPress build with a modern, mobile-first design and a clean technical foundation. Then the SEO engagement began.

- **Technical setup:** Analytics and Search Console, XML sitemap, robots.txt, metadata, plus image, CSS, JavaScript, and page-speed optimization so Google could read the site properly
- **Competitive landscape:** a competitor audit mapping where rivals rank and which gaps Lifecare can own, including its local-manufacturing moat
- **Content:** SEO-friendly page structure, a keyword-driven blog cluster, and internal linking
- **Local:** Google Business Profile built and optimized
- **Reporting:** a monthly progress report the client can actually read, metrics, context, and next steps

## Early signal (first month, Google Search Console)

- From **zero indexed target keywords** to content indexed and surfacing in search
- **113 impressions, 24 clicks, a 21.2% click-through rate** (when the site appears, people click)
- Average position **26.5 and climbing**

These are seed numbers, reported honestly. Impressions are the lever now, and the content and internal linking to move them are already in motion.
