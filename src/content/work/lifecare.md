## Why this case study is different

Most portfolio case studies show you the trophy. This one shows you the work, one month in, with real numbers small enough to be believable. I'm publishing it because how a marketer starts an engagement tells you more than a polished end result.

## Challenge

Lifecare had a website that ranked for none of its 30 target keywords. Zero. In a market where hospitals, pharmacies, and procurement teams search before they buy, the company was invisible.

## Approach, month one

- **Technical audit and fixes:** speed, crawlability, and schema markup, so Google could read the site at all
- **Competitive landscape:** a 40-competitor audit mapping where rivals rank and which gaps Lifecare can own, including its local-manufacturing moat
- **Keyword and content strategy:** a keyword-to-competitor matrix that decides every piece of content
- **Editorial production:** a 10-post blog cluster on the topics its buyers search, from import regulation to cold-chain handling
- **Google Business Profile:** built and optimized for local search
- **Reporting:** a monthly progress report the client can actually read, metrics, context, and next steps

## Early signal (first month, Google Search Console)

- From **zero indexed target keywords** to content indexed and surfacing in search
- **113 impressions, 24 clicks, a 21.2% click-through rate** (when the site appears, people click)
- Average position **26.5 and climbing**

These are seed numbers, reported honestly. Impressions are the lever now; the content volume and internal linking to move them are already in motion.
