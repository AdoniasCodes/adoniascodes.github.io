## Challenge

Eagle Eye is a marketing agency I helped build from the ground up in Ethiopia, a market where digital marketing was still winning over traditional businesses. As Marketing Director I owned strategy, client acquisition, and delivery across a small multidisciplinary team, while we also built our own products. The constant challenge of any young agency applied: win clients, run several accounts at once, and prove that modern marketing pays.

## Approach

I led multi-channel campaigns that mixed paid social and search with TV advertising and events, and I built or oversaw client websites with SEO baked in rather than bolted on. Every engagement started the same way, with research and clear campaign targets, then creative direction across designers, copywriters, and video editors so the work stayed consistent.

The flagship client win was **Palm Real Estate**. Their problem was lead generation, so I rebuilt the funnel: sharper targeting, better paid campaigns, and website and conversion fixes that turned traffic into enquiries.

We also built products of our own. **CarCare** was the biggest: Ethiopia's take on an AAA-style roadside-assistance membership, covering roadside help, a mobile garage, and vehicle services. I owned its positioning, marketing strategy, website, and launch campaigns.

## Result

- **Palm Real Estate: roughly 300% increase in lead generation** after the funnel and campaign rebuild
- **CarCare** launched from zero, validated the membership model, and reached **12 to 20 daily customers** in its first month
- Delivered client websites, SEO projects, and multi-channel campaigns across the agency's book
- Built the creative and campaign systems the team still runs

*This is where I stopped being a marketer for hire and started building businesses, not just marketing them.*
