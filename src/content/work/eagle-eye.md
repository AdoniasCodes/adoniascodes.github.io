## Challenge

Eagle Eye's clients were spending on ads without a system: no consistent creative testing, no cost-per-acquisition targets, and results that swung month to month. The group needed someone to direct paid acquisition across every client account and make the spend answer for itself.

## Approach

I took over paid social and paid search across Meta, Google, and TikTok Ads. Every account got the same discipline: clear CAC targets per offer, structured creative testing, and weekly reporting that tied spend to leads instead of impressions.

The sharpest test was a cold-start launch. Eagle Eye's CarCare service had zero customers and zero brand recognition. I ran the go-to-market end to end: offer framing, launch creative, geo-targeted paid campaigns, and a follow-up loop to turn first visits into repeat customers.

I also managed the team behind the work, 5 to 7 people across creative, media buying, and events, so campaigns shipped on schedule instead of when everyone was free.

## Result

- **30+ campaigns** executed across Meta, Google, and TikTok
- Client lead generation lifted by up to **300%**
- CarCare launch reached **12 to 20 daily customers** within the first month, from a standing start
- A **repeatable campaign playbook** the team still runs

*Every number above comes from campaign reporting I built myself. I bring the same dashboards to every engagement.*
